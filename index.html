How the rating is calculated

Placement rate: placements in rated years ÷ eligible graduates in those years × 100. College admins report each class size in their private workspace.

Package attainment: the average package ÷ ₹20 LPA, capped at 100%.

Final rating: 75% placement rate + 25% package attainment. The average and package contribution stay private until five placements in rated years; before that, the displayed rating is provisional and uses placement rate only. An asterisk marks a provisional rating.

Example: 120 of 200 graduates placed = 60%; an ₹8 LPA average = 40% of the package target. (0.75 × 60) + (0.25 × 40) = 55 / 100.

Colleges without a reported class size have no rating. Colleges with no placements in rated years are not ranked.

Ties are broken by placement rate, then rated placements, then average package, then college name.

Aggregate results only. Student names, college email IDs, class sizes, and individual package values are not shown; the public page shows the placement rate.
